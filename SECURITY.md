# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities by email to **security@liyagent.com**. Do not open a public issue.

Include the following in your report:

- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce it
- The version of Liyagent or of this repository that is affected

We acknowledge every report within three business days and keep you informed until the issue is resolved.

## Scope

This policy covers the code in this repository and the Liyagent platform. Product names of other vendors that appear in this repository are trademarks of their respective owners.
