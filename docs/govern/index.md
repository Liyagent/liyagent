---
title: Control & govern
order: 1
summary: Decide who may do what, what agents may do on their own, where data may go and how it is protected.
---

Governance in Liyagent is enforced on the server, on every request. **Roles** say what a person may do in the console. **Cedar policies** say what any principal, person or agent, may do to any resource. **Tenants** keep customers or business units apart. **Redaction and sensitivity labels** keep data out of places it shouldn't reach. The audit log records all of it.
